🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Xencard Keeper is built with a privacy-first, offline-first architecture. Your business card data stays on your device by default. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

Xencard Keeper is designed to work entirely offline. The app processes and stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Business card images — captured via your device camera
  • Contact information — names, phone numbers, emails, addresses, company names, and job titles extracted from scanned cards
  • Personal card profiles — your own business card information that you create within the app

2. Camera Permission

The app requests camera access solely for scanning business cards. Images are processed on-device using OCR (Optical Character Recognition) and are never uploaded to external servers unless you explicitly enable cloud sync.

3. On-Device Processing

All OCR text extraction happens directly on your device using:

  • Google ML Kit (Android) — on-device text recognition
  • Apple Vision Framework (iOS) — on-device text recognition

No images or extracted text are sent to any cloud service for processing by default.

4. Data Storage

All data is stored locally in an on-device SQLite database. Your contacts, card images, and personal information remain on your device at all times unless you choose to enable optional cloud synchronization.

5. Optional Cloud Sync

If you choose to enable cloud synchronization (optional feature), your data may be securely transmitted and stored on our cloud servers. Cloud sync is:

  • Entirely optional and disabled by default
  • Requires explicit user action to enable
  • Uses industry-standard encryption for data in transit and at rest

6. Third-Party Services

The app does not include:

  • ❌ Advertising SDKs or trackers
  • ❌ Analytics or telemetry services
  • ❌ Social media tracking pixels

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to third parties. Contact sharing features (VCF export, QR codes) are initiated only by your explicit action.

8. Data Deletion

You can delete any or all of your data at any time:

  • Delete individual contacts from within the app
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data

9. Children's Privacy

Xencard Keeper is a business productivity tool designed for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
